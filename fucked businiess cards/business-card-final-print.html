Print Specifications

Dimensions

  • Finished Size: 3.5" × 2" (standard business card)
  • With Bleed: 3.75" × 2.25" (0.125" bleed all sides)
  • Safe Zone: 3.25" × 1.75" (keep text/logo inside)

Material Specifications

  • Stock: 16pt black uncoated cardstock
  • Lamination: 1.5mil soft-touch/velvet matte coating (both sides)
  • Total Thickness: ~19pt
  • Foil: Hot stamped silver foil

Color Requirements

  • Background: Rich Black (40C/30M/30Y/100K)
  • Foil Elements: Provided as separate K=100 layer
  • Color Space: CMYK
  • Resolution: 300 DPI minimum

Typography

  • Fonts: Helvetica Neue (convert to outlines)
  • Letter Spacing: 110-120% for optimal legibility on dark background
  • Minimum Size: 9pt

Quantity & Delivery

  • Quantity: 500 cards
  • Turnaround: 5-7 business days (standard)
  • Packaging: Shrink-wrapped in boxes of 250

Recommended Printers

  • Gold Image Printing - Specialty foil experts
  • Jukebox Print - Premium soft-touch finishes
  • PSPrint - Reliable, good pricing
  • Moo - High-end quality, faster turnaround

Files to Submit

  • Main file: Full color PDF with bleed (front & back)
  • Foil layer: K=100 black PDF showing foil placement (front & back)
  • Technical spec sheet: This document as reference

Pre-Flight Checklist

  • ✓ All fonts converted to outlines
  • ✓ Images embedded (not linked)
  • ✓ Color mode set to CMYK
  • ✓ Resolution 300 DPI or higher
  • ✓ Bleed extended 0.125" on all sides
  • ✓ Critical elements inside safe zone
  • ✓ Foil layer on separate file
  • ✓ PDF/X-1a:2001 compliant (recommended)
