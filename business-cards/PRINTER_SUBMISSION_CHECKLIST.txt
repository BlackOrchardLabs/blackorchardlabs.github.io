BUSINESS CARD STORAGE
---------------------
✓ Keep in:
  - Business card holder/case
  - Dry environment
  - Away from direct sunlight
  - Original boxes when not in use

✗ Avoid:
  - Rubber bands (can damage soft-touch)
  - Wallet (can scratch foil)
  - Humid environments
  - Excessive handling
